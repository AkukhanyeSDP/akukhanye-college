About Akukhanye

Bringing light to communities through quality education since 2010

Our Story

Born from community need

Akukhanya — meaning "light" in isiXhosa and isiZulu — was founded with a singular purpose: to bring quality further education to communities in the Eastern Cape that had long been overlooked by traditional institutions.

Since our founding, we have grown from a small training centre into a fully accredited private college offering 16 programmes across NQF Levels 4 and 5, with a rapidly expanding footprint across the Eastern Cape.

Our Purpose

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality education and skills training that prepares students for meaningful employment and entrepreneurship, while contributing to the social and economic development of the Eastern Cape and South Africa.

Our Vision

To be a leading private college in Pondoland and beyond, known for producing work-ready, competent graduates who drive change in their communities and industries.
